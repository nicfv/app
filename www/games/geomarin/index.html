GeoMarin

The hint below describes a location found in Marin, California. Try to guess where it can be found within 5 guesses or less. To make a guess, click on the cities on the right, or click directly on the map. Making one guess at a time will color one of the 5 circles at the bottom of the map. An incorrect guess will color a circle in gray or yellow and the correct guess will color a circle green. A gray circle means that your guess does not border the correct guess at all, but a yellow circle means that your guess shares at least one border with the correct guess (including corners.) Good luck, and enjoy discovering new places in Marin!
