Orbit Idle

This game takes a new spin as the 4th game in the Revolution Idle series.

Controls: Mouse/click for everything. Instructions are shown in-game.

Hotkeys: Zoom: + and -   Save: S   Stats: A   Mute/Unmute: M   Help: H   Menu: ESC

The original game: Revolution Idle (2017)

Fan collab/remake: Revolution Idle 2 (2017)

My Unity3D remake: Idle R3volutions (2018)

Fun fact: Idle R3volutions was available on Android/Google Play for a short time!
